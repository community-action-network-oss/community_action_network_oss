# OQ-capability-taxonomy: Which plain-language groups describe what people know?

- **ID:** OQ-capability-taxonomy
- **Status:** open

## Question

Which groups does the capability profile offer for "what you know", and who keeps the list current?

## Why it matters

The groups are the only vocabulary shared by the profile and by `help_needed` on public problems. Groups that are too coarse match nothing useful, and groups that are too fine or too jargon-heavy shut out people who do not use job titles.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

The plain-language groups in spec 26, extended through policy packs so a place or field can add its own. No job titles, no employers.

## Who can help

People from every profession, plain-language writers, translators, and people who do not think of what they do as a profession.

## What a good answer looks like

A list people from many walks of life recognise themselves in, tested with real readers, with a rule for how a pack adds or retires a group.

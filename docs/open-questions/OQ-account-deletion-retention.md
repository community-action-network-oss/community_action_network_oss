# OQ-account-deletion-retention: What happens when someone deletes their account?

- **ID:** OQ-account-deletion-retention
- **Status:** open

## Question

What is removed, what is kept and why when an account is deleted, given that public records and audit trails exist?

## Why it matters

People have a right to leave. The public record and moderation audit also have value and legal limits.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

Email and sessions are erased. Drafts are hard-deleted. Published contributions stay under a "deleted member" label with the handle de-linked. Audit events keep a pseudonymous id.

## Who can help

Privacy lawyers; archivists; trust-and-safety practitioners.

## What a good answer looks like

A table of data types with keep, anonymize or delete, and the reason for each.

# OQ-location-verification: How is a person's connection to an area established and shown?

- **ID:** OQ-location-verification
- **Status:** open

## Question

How should the platform express and (if at all) verify that someone is materially connected to the affected area, without GPS, tracking or exclusion?

## Why it matters

It decides who counts as a core participant and whose voice shapes a decision. Wrong answers either leak location or let outsiders steer local problems.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

A self-declared coarse area, for display only, never verified. Nobody is excluded in slice 1. No GPS is collected.

## Who can help

Privacy researchers; GIS and geography specialists; civic participation researchers; accessibility reviewers.

## What a good answer looks like

A proposal of 2 or 3 options with their privacy cost, abuse risk and who they exclude, and a recommended default.

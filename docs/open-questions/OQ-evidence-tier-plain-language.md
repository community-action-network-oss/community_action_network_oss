# OQ-evidence-tier-plain-language: How do we explain evidence tiers and the investigation flag to ordinary people?

- **ID:** OQ-evidence-tier-plain-language
- **Status:** open

## Question

What plain wording explains evidence tiers and the `investigation_needed` flag without jargon?

## Why it matters

Misreading "needs investigation" as "false" or "established" as "proven" harms trust.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

A neutral chip, "Evidence still being gathered", with a one-sentence explanation and a link to what would help.

## Who can help

Plain-language writers; UX researchers; accessibility reviewers.

## What a good answer looks like

Tested wording for each tier in at least two reading levels.

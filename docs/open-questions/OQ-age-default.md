# OQ-age-default: What is the minimum age?

- **ID:** OQ-age-default
- **Status:** open

## Question

What minimum age applies, how is it confirmed, and what may a younger person do?

## Why it matters

Rules differ by jurisdiction. The platform must not collect more data than it needs to check age.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

18 or over, self-declared at sign-up. A capability with no age rule in the active jurisdiction pack is denied except public reading.

## Who can help

Child-safety specialists; legal reviewers; educators.

## What a good answer looks like

A recommendation for one jurisdiction with the evidence, and what a youth-friendly reading experience could be.

# OQ-human-presence: How do we limit bots and fake accounts without collecting identity?

- **ID:** OQ-human-presence
- **Status:** open

## Question

What mechanism, beyond an invite, an email code and rate limits, could show that an account is a real person without identity documents or device tracking?

## Why it matters

Fake accounts distort every discussion. Identity checks create privacy risk and exclude people.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

Invite-only writes, email code sign-in, rate limits. No device identifiers or biometrics.

## Who can help

Anti-abuse engineers; privacy researchers; accessibility reviewers.

## What a good answer looks like

A shortlist of mechanisms with their privacy cost, who they exclude and how they can be defeated.

# OQ-rights-core-amendment: How can the protected rights core ever be amended?

- **ID:** OQ-rights-core-amendment
- **Status:** open

## Question

The constitution protects a rights core that ordinary amendments cannot remove. Should there be an extraordinary procedure, and what would it require?

## Why it matters

No procedure can mean the rule is unfixable if it is wrong. An easy one defeats the protection.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

No procedure. The core cannot be changed by the ordinary amendment process, and the founder cannot change it.

## Who can help

Constitutional scholars; governance designers; human-rights lawyers.

## What a good answer looks like

A draft procedure with thresholds, waiting periods and who must take part, and how it could be abused.

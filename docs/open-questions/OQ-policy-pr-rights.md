# OQ-policy-pr-rights: Who may open policy PRs, and who maintains can_policy?

- **ID:** OQ-policy-pr-rights
- **Status:** open

## Question

Who may open pull requests against the `can_policy` repository, who reviews them, and who maintains the repository?

## Why it matters

Open proposal rights keep the law community-made. Maintenance rights decide who can merge or stall changes.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

Anyone may propose. Appeals open PRs automatically. The founder is the sole maintainer under transitional stewardship (VIII.2) until maintainers beyond the founder are named. Merge requires the ratification step (`OQ-ratification-method`).

## Who can help

Open-source governance practitioners; community organizers.

## What a good answer looks like

A maintainer role description, how maintainers are added and removed, conflict rules, and anti-spam limits for proposals.

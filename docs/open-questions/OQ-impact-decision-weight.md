# OQ-impact-decision-weight: May the impacted label ever carry decision weight?

- **ID:** OQ-impact-decision-weight
- **Status:** open

## Question

Should an impacted label ever give a contribution more weight in a stage decision, and if so how?

## Why it matters

Weight could protect affected people from outsiders, or let a brigade game the label.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

Out of scope for slice 1. The label only filters and labels. It never changes moderation, and the stage decision method gives it no weight (`OQ-stage-decision-method`).

## Who can help

Governance and deliberation researchers; community organizers; affected-community representatives.

## What a good answer looks like

A bounded proposal with the failure cases and a way to detect gaming.

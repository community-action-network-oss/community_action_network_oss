# OQ-launch-jurisdiction-language: Where and in what language does the first real pilot run?

- **ID:** OQ-launch-jurisdiction-language
- **Status:** open

## Question

Amsterdam (Netherlands) is the first real jurisdiction overlay for the seed problems (D-56). Is it also the place of the first controlled pilot, and is the first language English, Dutch, or both?

## Why it matters

It decides the policy rule set, emergency routing, legal reviewers, moderation language evaluation and who the first users are. It must be a place where qualified reviewers exist.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

Amsterdam (NL) overlay for seeds 1 and 2, with synthetic evidence only, English first. Seed pages are labelled "Seed problem, synthetic evidence". Dutch-language support and moderation evaluation in Dutch are not built yet; non-English text is held as unsupported (`OQ-unsupported-language`). Real participants wait for graduation (`SIM-GATE-1`).

## Who can help

Civic practitioners; local-government liaisons; legal and policy reviewers; translators.

## What a good answer looks like

A short brief for 1 to 3 candidate places: available reviewers, language needs, a plausible first problem category, and risks.

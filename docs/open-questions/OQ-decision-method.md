# OQ-decision-method: How are solutions selected, and how are stewardship groups formed?

- **ID:** OQ-decision-method
- **Status:** open

## Question

What decision method turns proposals into a chosen solution, and how do stewardship groups form, reach quorum and handle dissent?

## Why it matters

Majority voting is not assumed to be right. The method decides local legitimacy, expert weight and minority rights.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

A recorded decision with rationale, decider, authority and dissent notes. No vote. The initiator is provisional steward and a moderator confirms publish, solved, closed and redirected.

## Who can help

Governance and deliberation researchers; facilitators; mediators; community organizers.

## What a good answer looks like

A written method for one problem type with worked fictional examples and failure cases.

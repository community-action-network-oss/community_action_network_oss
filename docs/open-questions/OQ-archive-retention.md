# OQ-archive-retention: How do withdrawal and account deletion interact with a permanent archive?

- **ID:** OQ-archive-retention
- **Status:** open

## Question

The Archive is a permanent public record. What happens to archived content when a poster withdraws or deletes their account?

## Why it matters

People must be able to leave without erasing the path others rely on, and without their personal text staying public.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

The archive is permanent. Withdrawal and account deletion tombstone the author's own text (`OWN-1`, Constitution II.8) and keep the path structure: stages, options, choices, evidence references, challenges and outcome.

## Who can help

Data-protection officers; archivists; privacy lawyers.

## What a good answer looks like

A rule for what stays and what is removed on request, how erasure requests are handled for a public record, and any jurisdiction limits.

# OQ-legal-data-requests: How does the project answer legal or government requests for data?

- **ID:** OQ-legal-data-requests
- **Status:** open

## Question

What is the process, and who decides, when a court, police force or government asks for user data or for content to be removed?

## Why it matters

Email addresses and moderation records could be demanded. A policy written after the first request is too late.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

No process yet. Slice 1 holds only fictional data. No real user data may be collected until a policy exists.

## Who can help

Lawyers; digital-rights organizations; people who have written transparency reports.

## What a good answer looks like

A draft policy: what is refused by default, who reviews a request, what is disclosed to affected people, and a transparency report outline.

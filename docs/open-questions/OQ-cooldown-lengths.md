# OQ-cooldown-lengths: How long should the reflection delays be?

- **ID:** OQ-cooldown-lengths
- **Status:** open

## Question

What delays between contributions, and after a rejected contribution, encourage thoughtful posting without silencing urgent updates?

## Why it matters

A flat wait punishes everyone. A well-aimed delay calms a heated exchange.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

2 minutes between contributions by one account on one problem; 10 minutes after a rejected contribution on that problem; none for progress updates and verification evidence.

## Who can help

Community managers; behavioural researchers; UX researchers.

## What a good answer looks like

A proposal with any evidence from comparable communities, and a way to measure whether it helps.

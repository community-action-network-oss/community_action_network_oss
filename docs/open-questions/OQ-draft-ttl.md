# OQ-draft-ttl: Are the draft retention numbers right?

- **ID:** OQ-draft-ttl
- **Status:** open

## Question

Are 30 days deletion for rejected or withdrawn drafts, 90 days for the repost fingerprint, 14 days to appeal, and 30 days before an idle `needs_revision` draft is withdrawn the right numbers?

## Why it matters

Deleting too fast hurts people who want to appeal or revise; keeping too long holds personal text.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

The numbers above. The UI shows the exact deletion date.

## Who can help

Privacy specialists; data-protection officers; UX researchers.

## What a good answer looks like

A recommendation per number with the reason, and any legal minimum or maximum for a named jurisdiction.

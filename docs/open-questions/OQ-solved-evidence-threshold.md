# OQ-solved-evidence-threshold: What evidence is enough to call a problem solved?

- **ID:** OQ-solved-evidence-threshold
- **Status:** open

## Question

What kinds and amounts of verification evidence let the moderation run decide `solved` (`DP-VERIFICATION`)?

## Why it matters

Declaring victory too early damages trust. Too strict a bar means nothing is ever solved.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

The steward proposes with at least one `verification_evidence` URL and an outcome statement against the success metric. The moderation run decides and cites the policy version. The threshold is a policy-pack setting.

## Who can help

Evaluation and audit specialists; public-administration researchers; journalists.

## What a good answer looks like

A short rubric with 3 fictional examples that pass and 3 that fail.

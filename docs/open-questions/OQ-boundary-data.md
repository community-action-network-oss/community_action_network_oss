# OQ-boundary-data: Which boundary data and licence define affected areas?

- **ID:** OQ-boundary-data
- **Status:** open

## Question

Which boundary dataset supplies administrative areas for affected areas, and under what licence and attribution?

## Why it matters

Areas should be reproducible, not drawn by one person, and the data must be legal to reuse.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

OpenStreetMap administrative boundaries (ODbL) with attribution, recorded as a `source_ref`. Posters may also draw an area, which volunteers can review.

## Who can help

Open-data practitioners; GIS specialists; licensing experts.

## What a good answer looks like

A dataset choice per launch jurisdiction, the attribution text, and how updates become new area versions.

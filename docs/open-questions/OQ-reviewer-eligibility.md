# OQ-reviewer-eligibility: Who may be a volunteer reviewer?

- **ID:** OQ-reviewer-eligibility
- **Status:** open

## Question

Who may opt in as a volunteer reviewer, and what conflict-of-interest rules apply?

## Why it matters

Reviewers see private problems before publication. Too open a door invites sabotage or brigading. Too closed a door leaves problems without review.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

Any signed-in member who opts in (a revocable account flag). Personal data is masked. Each reviewer self-declares any conflict of interest on a problem before reviewing it, and a poster never reviews their own problem. Spam and abuse are limited by per-day caps and `DP-TONE` and `DP-PRIVACY` on recommendations.

## Who can help

Trust and safety practitioners; community moderators; privacy specialists; governance researchers.

## What a good answer looks like

A written eligibility rule, a conflict-of-interest checklist, a way to limit brigading without identity checks, and what happens to a reviewer who breaks the rules.

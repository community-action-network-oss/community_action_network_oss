# OQ-steward-entity-grants: May a steward entity receive grants without breaking the non-monetary rule?

- **ID:** OQ-steward-entity-grants
- **Status:** open

## Question

Can a foundation, association or cooperative that stewards CAN's assets and governance apply for grants (for example Dutch digital-infrastructure or effective-giving programs listed as input in the earlier planning notes) without conflicting with `MONEY-0`, `NONMONETARY-INDEPENDENT` and supporter independence?

## Why it matters

The constitution forbids payments, donations and paid placement inside the product. A funder could still capture the project through a grant. The project also needs some way to pay for hosting and model spend beyond the founder.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

No fundraising, no grant applications, no entity. `MONEY-0` stays: no payment SDK or money columns in any build. Any funding discussion happens outside the product, in public, and is decided by founder then governance (`docs/spec/20-participation-nonmonetary.md`).

## Who can help

Nonprofit lawyers; open-source foundation staff; funders; governance researchers.

## What a good answer looks like

A model that separates entity-level funding from the product (disclosure, no say in policy, caps per funder, refusal rules), and which grant types are compatible.

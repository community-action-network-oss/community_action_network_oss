# OQ-legal-policy-reviewers: Who can review and approve jurisdiction policy packs?

- **ID:** OQ-legal-policy-reviewers
- **Status:** open

## Question

Which qualified legal and safety people will review policy packs and escalation procedures for a real jurisdiction?

## Why it matters

A policy pack states what is lawful to discuss and do. Without qualified review the platform must not claim legal grounding.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

No real pack exists. The fictional jurisdiction uses a rule set written by the project and marked as fictional.

## Who can help

Lawyers; legal-aid workers; academics in public law, data protection or election law.

## What a good answer looks like

A named reviewer pool for one jurisdiction, a review process, and a conflict-of-interest disclosure format.

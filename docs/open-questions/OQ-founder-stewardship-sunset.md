# OQ-founder-stewardship-sunset: When does transitional founder stewardship end?

- **ID:** OQ-founder-stewardship-sunset
- **Status:** open

## Question

The founder acts as a time-limited steward until a quorum exists. What exactly ends it?

## Why it matters

Without a clear end the arrangement looks permanent, and nobody can hold it to account.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

It ends when at least 5 independent stewards exist, or after 24 months, whichever comes first. Every founder approval is logged publicly with an expiry.

## Who can help

Governance designers; open-source foundation staff; community organizers.

## What a good answer looks like

A recommendation on the numbers, who counts as independent, and what happens at the end.

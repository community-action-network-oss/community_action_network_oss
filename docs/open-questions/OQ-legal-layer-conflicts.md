# OQ-legal-layer-conflicts: What happens when human rights (L1) conflict with national law (L3 or L4)?

- **ID:** OQ-legal-layer-conflicts
- **Status:** open

## Question

When a national constitution or statute conflicts with UN human rights instruments, or a topic ban conflicts with expression rights at L1 or L2, which prevails for a given decision, and who decides a contested conflict?

## Why it matters

The layers are cumulative so CAN never requests anything illegal, but a regime may forbid what rights instruments protect. Handling this wrongly either makes CAN assist unlawful action or silences protected speech.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

Cumulative restriction: content and solutions must satisfy every layer, so the stricter layer applies. The platform never assists illegal action. The refusal is logged with layer, source and version, the person gets a plain explanation and an appeal, and an unresolved conflict is held and queued as a policy question (`PREC-1`, `TOPIC-FORBIDDEN-1`). The rights-core protections in Constitution I.2 stay in force.

## Who can help

International human-rights lawyers; constitutional scholars; digital-rights organizations; local counsel.

## What a good answer looks like

A decision rule with worked examples, including a topic ban that conflicts with L1 or L2, and the escalation path for a contested case.

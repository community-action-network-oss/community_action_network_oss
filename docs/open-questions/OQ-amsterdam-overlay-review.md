# OQ-amsterdam-overlay-review: Who reviews the Amsterdam overlay, and which local partners help?

- **ID:** OQ-amsterdam-overlay-review
- **Status:** open

## Question

Who legally and civically reviews the Amsterdam (Netherlands) jurisdiction overlay used by seeds 1 and 2 (public safety, explosions and violence; city-centre cleanliness), and which Dutch partners (municipal liaisons, legal and policy reviewers, civic groups) can advise?

## Why it matters

Amsterdam is the first real jurisdiction overlay (D-56). Seeds touch policing, municipal duties and personal safety. A wrong overlay produces wrong lawfulness checks in the simulation and later in public use. The overlay is real law applied to synthetic evidence.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

The overlay is drafted from public sources by the project, marked as unreviewed, approved only by founder stewardship (`FOUNDER-TRANS-1`), and used only on synthetic evidence. No real incident, person or dataset enters. Public participation stays closed until the overlay has a qualified reviewer or is explicitly labeled unreviewed (`OQ-legal-policy-reviewers`).

## Who can help

Dutch lawyers and municipal-law specialists; Amsterdam civic organizations; public-safety and urban-cleanliness researchers; Dutch translators.

## What a good answer looks like

A named reviewer or partner, a checklist of overlay sources with effective dates, and whether Dutch or English or both are used (`OQ-launch-jurisdiction-language`).

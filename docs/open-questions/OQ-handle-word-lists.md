# OQ-handle-word-lists: Are the handle word lists acceptable across cultures?

- **ID:** OQ-handle-word-lists
- **Status:** open

## Question

Are the curated word lists used to generate public handles free of offensive, confusing or culturally loaded combinations?

## Why it matters

A handle is a person's public face. A bad combination can embarrass or harm.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

English curated lists, one regenerate allowed before first publish.

## Who can help

Linguists; cultural reviewers; sensitivity readers.

## What a good answer looks like

A reviewed list with a documented process for adding and removing words.

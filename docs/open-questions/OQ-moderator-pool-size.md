# OQ-moderator-pool-size: How many moderators before interim labels can go?

- **ID:** OQ-moderator-pool-size
- **Status:** open

## Question

What is the smallest moderator pool that allows independent appeal review and removes the "Interim decision" label?

## Why it matters

With one moderator the same person decides and reviews the appeal.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

Decisions are labelled "Interim decision, will be re-reviewed" while the pool is below 2. With 2 or more, the appeal reviewer differs from the decider.

## Who can help

Community organizers; trust-and-safety practitioners; volunteer coordinators.

## What a good answer looks like

A proposal for the number, recruitment, training and conflict-of-interest rules.

# OQ-moderator-pool-size: How big must the emergency/legal lane, auditor and labeler pools be?

- **ID:** OQ-moderator-pool-size
- **Status:** open

## Question

Per-item moderators no longer exist (D-51). How many people does the project need for the emergency/legal lane, for auditing sampled decisions, and for label tasks, before the transitional marking can go?

## Why it matters

The lane is small but must be reachable. Auditors and labelers make label tasks independent. With too few, one person's view decides the label.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

AI decides single items. Decisions show "Policy vX, transitional stewardship" until a ratifying panel approves the pack (`INTERIM-1`). The founder covers the emergency/legal lane, and every lane action is logged (`NO-INSTANCE-OVERRIDE-1`). Label tasks are simulated in tests.

## Who can help

Community organizers; trust-and-safety practitioners; volunteer coordinators.

## What a good answer looks like

A proposal for the numbers per role, recruitment, training, hours of cover for the lane, and conflict-of-interest rules.

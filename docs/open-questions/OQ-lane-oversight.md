# OQ-lane-oversight: Who oversees the emergency and legal lane?

- **ID:** OQ-lane-oversight
- **Status:** open

## Question

The only human path left is the small emergency, crisis and legal lane (`LEGAL-LANE-1`, `NO-INSTANCE-OVERRIDE-1`). Who staffs it, who audits its actions, how are conflicts of interest handled, and what is published about its use?

## Why it matters

A lane that can act on single items is the single point where capture, error or abuse can bypass the community's policy. It also touches law enforcement requests and people in danger.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

Founder acts as the lane under transitional stewardship. Every action carries a logged reason, rule ids and time; aggregate use is published; a sample of lane actions goes to auditors. No dependence on the lane for ordinary moderation. See also `OQ-emergency-routing` and `OQ-legal-data-requests`.

## Who can help

Crisis and trauma-informed practitioners; lawyers; oversight and audit specialists; data-protection officers.

## What a good answer looks like

A staffing and rotation proposal, an audit method with sample sizes, a conflict-of-interest rule, and the contents of the public aggregate report.

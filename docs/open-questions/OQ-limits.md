# OQ-limits: What per-account limits apply to posting and appeals?

- **ID:** OQ-limits
- **Status:** open

## Question

Are the default per-account limits right: 3 problems per 7 days, 20 contributions per day with at most 8 per problem, 2 proposals per account per problem, 5 open drafts, and 3 appeals per 7 days?

## Why it matters

Limits slow floods and brigading and keep the pipeline affordable, but tight limits silence honest, active people. They also interact with the reflection delays in `OQ-cooldown-lengths`.

## Current default (what we built meanwhile)

The numbers above as pack values, applied equally to persona accounts in the simulation so the limits are tested before real people meet them. Progress updates and verification evidence are not counted against the contribution limit.

## Who can help

Community managers; abuse and anti-spam engineers; behavioural researchers; accessibility specialists.

## What a good answer looks like

A reasoned table of limits by action, an exception rule for urgent updates, and evidence from simulation or pilot runs.

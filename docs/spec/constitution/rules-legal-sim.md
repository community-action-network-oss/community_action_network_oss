# Rule registry: legal layers and simulation

Continuation of [`rules.md`](rules.md) (same shape and same rules, split only to keep each file at 25KB or less): the legal layer stack and legal-corpus rules (D-59, D-61), the emergency and legal lane, and the persona simulation rules (D-55 to D-57), the location label rules (D-73) and the archive and reuse rules (D-76). `tools/check.mjs` reads both files.

| RULE-ID | Source | Trigger | Must / must-not | Evidence | Test | First phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SIM-GATE-1 | V.5, VIII.2 | Public participation (real members posting) is about to open, or a pack change alters a graduation-relevant decision point | Must not open public writes until criteria G1 to G13 (`docs/design/ai/simulation.md` section 8, values held in the ratified pack) hold on a recorded persona-simulation run set of seeds 1 and 2, with run ids. Must re-run the simulation after a pack change touching those decision points before it goes live. | Graduation record: criteria version, run ids, results, approver | Test: with a failing or missing graduation record the invite and sign-up write path stays off. A passing record on FakeModel enables it in the fixture | S1 |
| LEGAL-LANE-1 | IV.5, V.4 | `escalate_human` is returned for an emergency, crisis, law enforcement or unresolved legal-lawfulness case | Must route only to the small emergency and legal lane, which acts within a logged reason, rule ids and time. Must not use the lane for ordinary moderation. Must publish aggregate lane use | Lane audit event and the aggregate report | Test: ordinary decision points cannot return `escalate_human` except for the listed triggers. A lane action without a logged reason is refused | S1 |
| SIM-LABEL-1 | V.5 | A persona identity, content item or seed evidence exists | Must be labeled synthetic ("Seed problem, synthetic evidence", a persona marker) on every surface and export. Must never reach a public surface as real or count as real participation. | Synthetic flag on the row and in the API response | Test: every response and export with a seeded or persona row carries the flag. Real-participation metrics exclude them | S1 |
| SIM-NOSECRET-1 | V.5, V.2 | A persona agent is built or run | Must hold no production secrets and no tools beyond the API client. Must act only through the public API as an ordinary account. Must not touch the database or the policy repo directly. | Persona definition and run config | Lint: a persona definition with a secret, a tool or a direct database handle fails. A persona run uses the same routes and rate limits as a person | S1 |
| LEGAL-STACK-1 | I.2, IV.5, IV.6 | Any moderation run, and every decision point that judges legality (`DP-LEGALITY`, `DP-ELIGIBILITY`, `DP-DECISION-RECORD`, `DP-RERESOLUTION`) | Must apply all legal layers cumulatively for the jurisdiction: L0 CAN rules, L1 UN human rights, L2 supranational where binding, L3 national constitution, L4 national law, L5 regional, L6 city. Must cite the layer and source for each finding. Must not skip a layer because another is silent, or let a lower layer relax a higher one. | Decision row with `rule_ids` and per-layer findings (layer, source id, version) | Table-driven test: a fixture failing only at L4 or only at L6 is blocked. A missing layer corpus holds the run (`FAIL-CLOSED-AI-1`) | S1 |
| LEGAL-SOURCE-1 | IV.6 | A legal corpus is added, updated or loaded for a layer | Must carry provenance: official publisher, URL, original text, language, layer, effective date, retrieval date, version and content hash. Must load by version and hash. Must not be rewritten by an LLM. | Corpus manifest per layer and verification record | Test: a corpus missing provenance or a matching hash fails to load | S1 |
| TOPIC-FORBIDDEN-1 | IV.5, I.2 | Local law of a jurisdiction forbids discussing the topic of a problem at all | Must not publish the problem in that jurisdiction. Must log the refusal with its legal basis (layer, source id, version) and show a plain explanation and an appeal path. Must not apply this when only a solution is illegal: then the problem is published and the stage is `blocked` or the problem `stuck` (legally blocked, `BLOCKER-1`). | Refusal record with legal basis, or the `blocked` or `stuck` record | Fixtures: a forbidden topic is refused with a logged basis. An illegal-only proposal reaches `blocked` | S1 |
| LEGAL-CITE-1 | IV.5, IV.6, V.4 | Any decision that rests on a legal finding | Must cite, for each finding, the layer (L0 to L6), the article or provision, and the legal-corpus version. Must not decide on law without a hash-verified corpus. | Decision row with layer, article, corpus version | DB constraint test: a legality decision missing any of them fails | S1 |
| LEGAL-CORPUS-1 | IV.6, V.5 | A legal corpus is proposed or changed (any layer) | Must carry a lawyer review record before ratification. A ratified change must trigger re-moderation of published content and re-resolution review (`RERESOLVE-1`). Must not go live on ingestion alone or by LLM rewrite. A conflict between layers is held with a conflict note, never silently resolved. | Review record, ratification entry, re-resolution job id | Test: a corpus PR without a review record cannot ratify. A ratified fixture change enqueues both jobs | S1 |
| IMPACT-1 | IV.2 | A contribution (any type, any stage) is submitted | Must set `impact_label` (`impacted` or `guest`) from the private attestation result at send time and the problem's `area_version`. Must label per message, not per profile. Must default to `guest` on a missing, stale or invalid attestation. Must accept the message either way. | `impact_label`, `attestation_result`, `area_version`, `proof_type` on the contribution row | Fixtures: inside proof gives `impacted`. No permission, outside, stale and a verifier outage give `guest` and a posted message. The same account labelled differently in two places | S1 |
| GUEST-LABEL-1 | IV.2 | Any content list or view of a problem is rendered | Must show a visible label on every guest contribution, offer an "Impacted only" filter on every content list, and keep the filter off by default. The filter must hide, never delete or alter, guest content. | Label in the API response and rendered chip; filter parameter on list routes | UI and route test: guest items always carry the label. Toggling the filter changes the view only, and the stored rows stay unchanged | S1 |
| LOC-PRIV-1 | II.2, II.6 | An attestation is received, verified, logged or stored | Must not store or log coordinates, cells, geohashes or IP-derived location. Must verify proofs then discard them. Must redact the attestation payload from logs. Must keep the attestation out of every moderation run input. Must not expose a per-person list of areas where someone is impacted. | Schema (no location columns), log redaction config, run input schema | Schema scan: no location column besides label, result, area version and proof type. Log test: no attestation body appears. Moderation input fixture contains no location data | S1 |
| LOC-DOUBT-1 | II.2, IV.2 | A location signal is doubtful (mock-location flag, failed integrity token, implausible jump, rate surge) | Must downgrade only that message to `guest` with a neutral reason. Must not restrict, sanction, flag or reduce the reputation of the account on location signals alone, and must never say "suspected spoofing". | `attestation_result` with a coarse reason; absence of any restriction row from a location signal | Test: every doubt signal yields `guest` and leaves restriction and reputation rows unchanged | S1 |
| ARCHIVE-1 | IV.7 | A problem reaches `solved`, `closed`, `redirected`, `withdrawn` after publication, or `stuck` | Must build a public archive record with the full path, options and choices, evidence, failed paths and challenges, costs and outcome, through `DP-ARCHIVE`. Must strip personal data with the privacy gateway before it is public. Must not archive pre-publication problems. Must not rank or score records. A failed build holds and retries and leaves the problem's state unchanged. | `archive_record` row with `context_profile`, `executed_path`, `challenge` list and versions | Fixtures: each terminal state yields a record. A stuck or blocked stage yields a `challenge`. Seeded personal data never appears in a record. A rejected draft yields none | S1 |
| REUSE-CONTEXT-1 | IV.7 | A path suggestion is built or shown | Must show the context differences and the legality (L0 to L6 of the new problem) and resource-fit results from `DP-REUSE-FIT`. Must not adopt a suggestion automatically or hide one that fails legality. | `path_suggestion` with per-dimension similarity, differences and fit results | Test: a suggestion failing local legality is shown as unusable with the reason. No route adds suggested content without a poster action | S1 |
| REUSE-CREDIT-1 | IV.7 | A suggestion is shown or a stage plan or option is derived from archive records | Must show `attribution` to the source archive records and carry it into the derived stage plan. Must not strip the credit line. | `attribution` on the suggestion and on the derived plan | Test: a plan drafted from a suggestion carries its source references. Removing them is refused | S1 |
| REUSE-NOBLOCK-1 | IV.7, V.4 | Suggestions or AI drafts are used for a problem in preparation or review | Must still require at least one completed volunteer review (`REVIEW-1`). Must invite community input without making it a blocker. Must not treat strong archive evidence as a review. | Review records and the `DP-PUBLISH` inputs | Test: a problem with accepted suggestions and zero completed reviews stays `in_review`. Open community input does not block T04 once the review condition holds | S1 |
